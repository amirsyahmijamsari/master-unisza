# Thesis Structure & Coherence Reviewer

This skill guides the evaluation of chapter-by-chapter logical progression and structural cohesion across the thesis.

## Structural Audit Checklist

1. **Chapter Progression & Alignment**:
   - **Chapter 1 (Introduction)**: Problem formulation, specific research objectives (RO1, RO2, RO3), and scope.
   - **Chapter 2 (Literature Review)**: Critical evolution of flood frequency methods, transition from conventional PWM to L-moments, and temperate vs. tropical limitations.
   - **Chapter 3 (Methodology)**: Step-by-step analytical sequence from data acquisition (AMS and daily) $\rightarrow$ L-moments computation $\rightarrow$ 9-distribution parameter estimation $\rightarrow$ MADI/MSDI goodness-of-fit $\rightarrow$ quantile estimation $\rightarrow$ overestimation quantification.
   - **Chapter 4 (Research Gap)**: Clear delineation of existing limitations in AMS-only paradigms in tropical climates.
   - **Chapter 5 (Results & Discussion)**: Empirical results organized strictly parallel to research objectives; detailed overestimation tables and return period curves.
   - **Chapter 6 (Conclusion)**: Explicit synthesis answering each research objective with future recommendations.

2. **Section Transitions & Cohesion**:
   - Verify every chapter begins with an introduction outlining chapter scope and ends with a transition summary into the next chapter.
   - Ensure the standard concluding reference disclaimer is present: *(See References chapter for complete reference list)*.
